Initial Recommendations

Highest-leverage actions — start here in the next 30 days

1

Rewrite homepage in 15 minutes or less

Above the fold: who you help, what you do, what they get, and one CTA. Use the formula: "I help [who] achieve [outcome] without [pain point]." This single change can double conversion rate.

Week 1Est. impact: +40% leads
2

Add a pricing page with at least 3 tiers

Even if prices are "starting from X", visibility removes friction. Use a 3-tier model: Starter / Growth / Scale. This qualifies leads before the call, saving 3–5 hours per week.

Week 1Est. impact: +25% call quality
3

Set up email capture + 5-email welcome sequence

Create a free lead magnet (PDF guide, checklist, or mini-course). Offer it in exchange for email. Use Mailchimp or ConvertKit. A 5-email nurture sequence turns cold traffic into warm leads on autopilot.

Week 2Est. impact: +60 leads/month
4

Unify all social bios to the same message

Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok bios should say the same thing with platform-appropriate length. Every bio should end with a single call to action pointing to the same link.

Week 2Est. impact: +20% profile conversions
5

Move testimonials to the homepage

Take your best 3 testimonials and place them prominently on the homepage and services page. Add a photo and name to each. Request video testimonials from your top 2 clients — these convert 3x better than text.

Week 3Est. impact: +30% trust score
